60-Day Action Plan (5 items)

1) Action: Launch MVP & Baseline Metrics
   - Owner: Product Manager
   - Expected business effect: Validate core value proposition; acquire first 100 users and initial revenue signals.
   - Risk: Low adoption or misaligned feature-market fit.
   - Success metric: 100 active users OR $2,000 MRR within 60 days.
   - Data needed for monitoring: user signups, DAU/WAU, conversion funnel, initial revenue (payments), acquisition channel tags.

2) Action: Improve Onboarding Conversion
   - Owner: Growth Lead
   - Expected business effect: Increase activation and retention, improving LTV/CAC economics.
   - Risk: Poor messaging changes can reduce conversions temporarily.
   - Success metric: Increase activation rate from baseline by 25% (e.g., activation from 20% to 25%).
   - Data needed for monitoring: funnel drop-off points, time-to-first-value, A/B test results, cohort retention.

3) Action: Stabilize Core Platform & Instrumentation
   - Owner: Engineering Lead
   - Expected business effect: Reduce errors and outages, improve user trust and retention.
   - Risk: Engineering time may delay new features.
   - Success metric: Reduce error rate by 50% and keep uptime >99.5%.
   - Data needed for monitoring: error/exception logs, uptime metrics, latency percentiles, SLO alerts.

4) Action: Run Top-Priority Growth Experiment(s)
   - Owner: Growth/Product Analyst
   - Expected business effect: Identify scalable acquisition channel or onboarding flow that drives sustainable growth.
   - Risk: Experiments may be inconclusive within 60 days.
   - Success metric: One experiment yields >2x ROI vs baseline channel or improves conversion by >=15%.
   - Data needed for monitoring: experiment cohort metrics, cost per acquisition, conversion rates, revenue per user.

5) Action: Customer Feedback & Roadmap Prioritization
   - Owner: Head of Product
   - Expected business effect: Prioritize high-impact features that increase retention and monetization.
   - Risk: Feedback may be biased or limited in sample size.
   - Success metric: Collect N>=30 qualitative interviews and prioritize top 3 roadmap items with estimated impact scores.
   - Data needed for monitoring: interview notes, CSAT/NPS, feature request frequency, qualitative themes.
